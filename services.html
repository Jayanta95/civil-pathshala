Planning Guide

Basic Vastu Tips We Follow

General guidelines used while planning — your plot may need custom solutions.

NE
Pooja Room & Entrance

North-East (Ishan) is ideal for pooja room, open space and main entrance.

SE
Kitchen

South-East (Agni) corner is best for kitchen; cook facing East.

SW
Master Bedroom

South-West gives stability — ideal for the master bedroom.

NW
Guest Room & Toilet

North-West suits guest bedrooms, toilets and store.

C
Brahmasthan

Keep the centre of the house open and free from heavy structures.

S/W
Staircase

Staircase in South, West or South-West, climbing clockwise.
