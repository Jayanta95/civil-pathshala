Our Story

Engineering Consultancy for Better Buildings

Civil Pathshala Engineering Consultancy is led by Pronab Choudhury, Civil Engineer & Planner. From our head office in Hakimpara, Siliguri and branch office in Islampur, Uttar Dinajpur, we help families and builders plan, design and construct safe, beautiful and budget-friendly buildings.

Through our YouTube channel, we also share practical civil engineering knowledge — house planning, structure, estimation and site work — in simple language, free for everyone.

  • Planning, 3D design, structure, approval & supervision under one roof
  • Practical, budget-friendly & Vastu-friendly planning
  • Local site support in Siliguri, Islampur & nearby areas
